🌟 About Our Club

The Fitness and Wellness Club is a student-centered initiative dedicated to promoting physical fitness, mental wellness, healthy habits and an active lifestyle among college students.

💪 Promote regular physical activity
🧘 Encourage mental wellness
🥗 Create awareness about healthy habits
🤝 Build teamwork and leadership

🎯 Vision & Mission

Vision: To create a healthy, energetic and positive college community.


Mission: To organize fitness activities, wellness programs, awareness campaigns, competitions and educational sessions that encourage students to maintain a balanced lifestyle.


Motto: “Move Today • Stronger Tomorrow”
